Sotto

A private AI note taker for your watch

Sotto turns a Wear OS watch into an always-on, speech-only note taker. The watch records when people talk. The phone turns each recording into a transcript with speakers, a summary and action items. Everything runs on your own devices. No account, no subscription, no cloud.

What it does

On the watch - Listens all day but saves only speech; silence is skipped. - Tile on the watch face shows live status and recording time. It also has Pause (15 min, 1 h or until resumed), Mark (bookmark an important moment) and Sync. - Sends recordings to the phone over a direct Bluetooth/Wi-Fi link only. - Keeps recordings safely on the watch until the phone confirms it received them.

On the phone - Notes: all recordings, grouped by day or place, with search, filters, star, rename, delete (with undo) and multi-select. - Transcripts with speakers (S1, S2…). Tap a line to play from that moment. Marks from the watch are highlighted. - AI summary and action items for every note, made on the phone by Gemma. - Ask your notes: ask a question about your conversations and get an answer with links to the notes it used. - Places: a map with your notes by location. Rename a place once and every note there follows. - Watch tab: watch status, battery, notes waiting, Start/Stop/Pause listening from the phone and Sync now. - Record with phone: use the phone's microphone for meetings (put it on the table). - Export & share: Markdown, PDF or text, to Gmail, Drive, Notion, NotebookLM, Files, or any app. - Open in Claude / Gemini: hands a note with a ready-made prompt to the official app, so your own subscription can analyse it. - App lock with fingerprint, face or PIN.

Requirements

Minimum
Phone Android 12 or newer, 64-bit (almost every phone since ~2018), ~4 GB free for AI models
Watch Wear OS 3 or newer with a microphone (Galaxy Watch4 and newer, Pixel Watch, TicWatch, OnePlus Watch…)
Not supported Apple Watch, Garmin, older Tizen Galaxy watches

The phone app also works without a watch, using Record with phone.

Install

1. Phone

  1. Copy Sotto-phone-1.0.apk to the phone (Drive, e-mail, USB cable…).
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for that app once.
  3. Open Sotto and allow notifications, location (optional, for Places) and the microphone when asked.

2. Watch

A watch can't open an APK file directly, so it is installed from a computer once: 1. On the watch: Settings → About watch → Software → tap "Software version" 5 times to unlock Developer options. 2. Settings → Developer options: turn on ADB debugging and Wireless debugging. Tap Pair new device and note the IP, port and code. 3. On the computer, using Android platform-tools (adb): adb pair <watch-ip>:<pair-port> # enter the code shown on the watch adb connect <watch-ip>:<port> adb install Sotto-watch-1.0.apk 4. Open Sotto on the watch, tap Start and allow the microphone and notifications. 5. Add the tile: swipe left on the watch face → + → Sotto.

Updating from an earlier test build? Uninstall the old Sotto first, because it was signed with a different key. Export your notes before uninstalling.

3. One-time AI setup (on the phone, Wi-Fi)

On the Notes screen, the AI card shows Download. It fetches the models once, about 3.2 GB: - Whisper turbo for transcription - Speaker detection to tell who is speaking - Gemma 4 E2B for summaries and Ask; E4B in Settings is slower but better

Downloads continue in the background, over Wi-Fi only.

Everyday use

I want to… Do this
Start listening Watch tile → Start, or phone Watch tab → Start listening
Pause for a meeting break Tile → ⏸ → 15 min / 1 h / until resumed
Remember an important moment Tile → 🔖 Mark (highlighted in the transcript)
Get the latest notes on the phone now Phone Notes → Sync, or Watch tab → Sync now
Record a meeting with the phone Notes → Record with phone → Start
Process notes right away AI card → Now. By default processing runs while charging.
Find something said last week Ask tab, e.g. "What did I promise Anna?"
Send a note to someone Open the note → Share, or long-press notes → Export
Get a deeper analysis Open the note → Open in Claude / Open in Gemini

Tip: leave the phone on the charger overnight and every note of the day is transcribed and summarised by morning.

Settings (gear on the AI card)

  • Languages you speak: transcription chooses only from these. The first one is the fallback.
  • Summary model and summaries in (English or the recording's language).
  • Only while charging (recommended).
  • Privacy: App lock; Street names for places (off by default, see below).
  • About: version and author.

Privacy

  • Recordings, transcripts, summaries and places are stored only on your phone and watch.
  • They are excluded from Google backup and from device-to-device transfer.
  • Transcription and AI run on the phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you share a note.
  • Watch → phone transfer uses a direct connection only, never Google's cloud relay.
  • Location uses the phone's own GPS only. Places are named on the phone. Street names (Settings → Privacy) are optional; turning them on sends coordinates to Google's address lookup.
  • App lock hides your notes from the recent-apps screen and blocks screenshots.
  • Network use (none of it personal):
  • One-time model downloads from GitHub and Hugging Face.
  • Map images from OpenFreeMap; they can see which map area is on screen.

Troubleshooting

Problem Fix
New notes don't appear Tap Sync. Check that the watch shows "Connected" and that Show all isn't hiding notes.
Watch says "Phone out of reach" Recordings stay safe on the watch and sync when the phone is back nearby.
"Start listening" from the phone shows a prompt on the watch Android lets only the watch itself switch its microphone on after a restart. Tap Start once on the watch.
Notes not transcribed Models downloaded? Phone charging (or tap Now)?
Lots of "Unknown place" Allow location all the time. GPS may not get a fix indoors.

For developers

  • Project: Android Studio, Kotlin 2.4, Jetpack Compose. Modules: app (Wear OS) and mobile (phone). Both use applicationId com.example.watchscribe.
  • Tests:
  • SottoLogicTest (phone) and WatchLogicTest (watch) check the logic.
  • ScreenshotsTest and WatchScreenshotTest regenerate docs/screens.
  • Run all of them with ./gradlew :mobile:testDebugUnitTest :app:testDebugUnitTest.
  • Release build: Build → Build App Bundle(s) / APK(s) → Build APK(s) with the release variant. It is signed with sotto-release.jks + keystore.properties. Keep both private and backed up. Updates must use the same key.
  • Key libraries:
  • sherpa-onnx for Whisper and speaker detection
  • LiteRT-LM for Gemma
  • MapLibre with OpenFreeMap for the map
